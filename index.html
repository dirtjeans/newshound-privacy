Newshound privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Newshound is a news reader. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The app doesn't collect your data, and its maker has no server that receives it. What you do in the app stays on your device, or in your own Google Drive folder if you turn on sync.

What stays on your device

  • The feeds and folders you follow, and your settings.
  • Which stories you've read, starred or saved for offline reading.
  • The town you choose for the weather, and the markets you choose to follow.

Removing the app deletes all of this.

What the app sends, and to whom

To show you the news, Newshound connects straight to other services. Each one sees your device's internet (IP) address, as any website you visit does. None of them gets your name, your email or an ID for you or your device.

These services have their own privacy policies. Newshound doesn't send them anything about which stories you read.

Sync

If you turn on sync, Newshound saves a small file in a Google Drive folder that you pick. The file lists your feeds and folders, and which stories you've read or starred. It goes through the Google Drive app on your device, under your own Google account. Newshound never sees your Google sign-in, and its maker can't read your Drive. To stop syncing, turn sync off and delete the folder.

Children

Newshound isn't made for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
